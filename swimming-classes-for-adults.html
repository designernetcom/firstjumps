Swimming Classes for Adults

Our adult swimming classes are designed to support learners of all experience levels through small, personalized, and self-paced sessions. Whether you are a complete beginner or looking to refine your technique, it is never too late to learn to swim. Even the most apprehensive adults can confidently overcome their fear of water in our safe, encouraging environment.

We provide access to one of the best swimming pools in Pune, offering specialized adult swimming programs alongside a large, well-maintained pool for kids and young adults. Our exclusive training structure also includes preschool swimming classes, ensuring age-appropriate learning across all groups. Each session is conducted with focused individual attention, allowing our coaches to deliver personalized instruction tailored to every swimmer’s needs.

Flexible Learning & Customized Programs

For adults who have never learned to swim or feel uncomfortable in the water, we offer both private and small group swimming sessions. A typical beginner program may include five 30-minute lessons per week (Monday to Saturday), with the flexibility to adjust class frequency according to your schedule and comfort level.

Program Highlights

  • Goal-Oriented Training
    Classes are structured around your personal objectives whether building water confidence, improving technique, fitness swimming, or preparing for advanced aquatic activities.
  • Positive & Individualized Coaching
    Supportive, professional instruction designed to motivate adults at every skill level.
  • Fear Reduction & Confidence Building
    The primary focus of our adult swimming academy is to eliminate fear and develop comfort through a self-paced, structured approach.
  • Experienced Instructors
    Our highly skilled coaches bring extensive experience working with swimmers of all abilities, ensuring safe and effective learning.

Swim with Purpose & Confidence

At Swim Now, we understand that every adult swimmer has different goals. Some seek relaxation and leisure swimming, while others aim for scuba diving, endurance swimming, or triathlon preparation. Our adult and young adult swimming programs are carefully customized to align with your aspirations.

No matter where you begin, you are in expert hands. What truly matters is the confidence, skill, and freedom you gain in the water. If you are looking for regular adult swimming classes in Pune, start your journey with us today and train at one of the finest swimming pools in Pune.
