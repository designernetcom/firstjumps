WHY CHOOSE US ?

Welcome to FIRST JUMP Swimming Academy

FIRST JUMP Swimming Academy offers professionally structured, small-group swimming programs led by certified instructors, designed to build confidence and achieve optimal performance outcomes for every child.

Through our purpose-designed facility, tailored programs, and experienced instructors, we are committed to providing an exceptional learning experience for every child.

All training is conducted in our purpose-designed, child-friendly facility, featuring year-round water temperatures maintained between 30–32°C and advanced systems that exceed all regulated standards for cleanliness and hygiene.

WHY FIRST JUMP IS THE BEST CHOICE FOR YOUR CHILD

Safe, professional and enjoyable swimming experience – all year round

Truly indoor above ground pool with warm water

With our truly indoor warm water pool you do not have to worry about Sun Burn or Rain or Cold. Your child will enjoy swimming in all seasons as the experience of swimming will be same in all weather conditions. Water temperature is maintained between 30 to 32 degrees Celsius. Hot and Cold water available in shower too.

Small class size and relax Ambiance

We have maximum 6 to 7 swimmers per coach in class (* exception summer season). As we teach professional swimming strokes and techniques, we maintain small class size. There are two classes conducted in pool at same time with max 14 students and 2 coaches (* exception summer season). Due to small class and indoor pool, we have less crowd and students can enjoy relaxed ambiance.

Unique Programs and Expert Teachers

Our programs are designed as per the age group. We maintain progress reports internally for each student to track and improve their strengths and weaknesses. Our coaches are well experienced and certified as trainers. We do conduct assessment events of student’s and certificate is provide post assessment.

High Hygiene Standard

As we have above ground indoor pool, we are less exposed to dust and outside elements. We use UV technology to disinfect the water as a result we can maintain optimum levels of Chlorine in water. Due to control on maximum number of people getting into pool every day we can also maintain water PH level to the optimum value.

Note: *Summer season is from March to June and during this time ratio for swimmers to coach can reach up to 8 students per coach.
