Health & Safety

At First Jump Swimming Academy, maintaining the highest standards of health and safety is our top priority.

We make every effort to keep our facilities safe, clean, and comfortable for both our members and staff. The following safety measures are implemented to ensure a healthy environment for all swimmers.

Our Filtration System

The pool filtration system plays a critical role in maintaining safe and healthy water. First Jump Swimming Academy uses the Minder Series of pool filters, a world leader in advanced filtration technology, capable of removing particles as small as 1 micron.

Minder UV disinfection systems enhance water quality and significantly reduce the need for chemical usage. This results in less surface and equipment corrosion and lowers the risk of pool closures.

Circulation & Turnover Rate

Effective water circulation and optimal turnover rates provide an additional layer of safety by reducing chlorine dependency. Turnover rate refers to the time required for 100% of the pool water to pass through the filtration system. Global health codes require a 6-hour turnover rate.

At First Jump Swimming Academy, our turnover rate is just 4 hours, exceeding global safety standards. Our advanced plumbing and equipment ensure that all pool water is continuously filtered for maximum cleanliness and safety.

Highly Trained Staff

Our full-time maintenance team is professionally trained and dedicated to monitoring and maintaining the pools and facilities seven days a week.

All First Jump team members receive comprehensive safety training, and deck managers actively supervise classes to ensure the highest levels of safety across all pool activities.
